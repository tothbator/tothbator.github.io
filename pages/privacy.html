PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how tóthbátor ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or subscribe to our newsletter.

Information We Collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. This information is collected through Klaviyo, our email marketing service provider.

How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to send you occasional updates about new work, exhibitions, and studio news. We send emails very infrequently and only when we have meaningful updates to share.

Data Protection

Your email address is stored securely by Klaviyo, which is compliant with GDPR and other data protection regulations. We do not share your email address with third parties, except for Klaviyo, which is necessary to provide the email service.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time

To exercise these rights or if you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@tothbator.com.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly at hello@tothbator.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
