VULCAN STUDIES

+ STORY

The VULCAN project started around the summer of 2024. By intense conversations about culture and certain ideologies that shape the times we live in; ideologies that shaped our past, misunderstood not so much for good but rather for bad as it is often the case. Met with the concerns of what this means for our future. I started to map out these ideas and wrap them into abstract allegories and metaphors. While all this on my mind already, in April 2025, I drew a sketch, drawn in a hospital visit, on a random piece of paper, with children's crayons. I loved it, and I wanted to expand on it so I started to do small scale studies with oil pastels - at this time I already heard VULCAN in the back of my mind. All this followed by months of research, taking notes, writing drafts and transferring the studies to canvas with oil sticks and oil paint.

The story of Vulcan, and other heroic journeys inspired me to think about longing for adventure, exile, hermitude; and the return - to oneself and into culture and society. What are the indestructable underlying elements of our existence that will always grow back with the same mechanisms. Are these unavoidable forever? Or does the future hold some extreme - yet unimaginable - transformation that relieves Humanity from it's hardships? Can we change? Can we change in ways that really matter?
